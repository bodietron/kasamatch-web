Delete your KasaM account

Last updated 12 September 2026.

KasaM is operated by Benito Concepcion.

To request deletion of your KasaM account and associated personal information, contact benlopcon+kasam@gmail.com. Include the email address used for your KasaM account and what you want deleted. These details help us locate your account; they do not by themselves verify ownership. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or ID images by email. We review requests manually and confirm ownership through your registered account contact before acting. If ownership cannot be confirmed, your request stays on hold while we assess account recovery.

The current iPhone beta, build 48, has no in-app account-deletion option. Android's existing in-app option may leave associated records behind; contact us for a request covering those records even if you have already used that option.

After confirmation, we review the requested records and any applicable retention, then process the agreed deletion actions and check their outcome. We explain what has been completed, what remains pending and what is retained, including the reason or next step. Closing your account does not by itself confirm that every associated record has been erased.
