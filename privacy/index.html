Privacy policy

Last updated 12 September 2026.

Who handles your information

KasaM is operated by Benito Concepcion, the personal information controller responsible for how your information is used. For privacy questions or requests, contact benlopcon+kasam@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

KasaM handles information you provide for your account, profile, and app activity. This includes your name, email address, phone number, profile information and uploaded images; location details and home pins you provide, including Google Maps links; helper profiles, rates and skills; and bookings, messages and reviews.

This information supports accounts and profiles, helper and household interactions, and the booking, messaging and review features you use. On iPhone, you can select an existing photo using the system photo picker.

KasaM uses Google Firebase services for authentication, app records, uploaded files, server functions and notifications. The Android app also includes Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics components. Firebase Analytics is not enabled in the current iPhone beta, build 48.

How long we keep information

We use the retention periods below to guide manual review and disposal. They are targets for handling the relevant records, not automatic expiry guarantees. We review information due for removal and record completed actions, justified retention and anything still pending. Where removal is unfinished, we explain the affected information and the next step when responding to your request.

Account and helper profiles, including profile photos

Retention target: Keep while account is active; remove in verified account-deletion workflow once scoped shared-record/hold decisions are resolved. Remove replaced photos when replacement succeeds.

Minimum retained and purpose: Only a restricted request reference and scoped completion evidence, under the request-log rule below.

Device tokens

Retention target: Remove on sign-out/revocation when identified; expire stale tokens after 90 days without activity; remove on verified account deletion.

Minimum retained and purpose: No token retained for analytics or proof of deletion.

Government-ID images, selfies and verification evidence

Retention target: Delete raw files 30 days after final verification decision; abandoned submissions after 30 days without response. Delete sooner through verified deletion where no specific hold applies. These windows allow correction/review, not reuse.

Minimum retained and purpose: While account active, keep decision/status, decision date, reviewer reference, policy version and brief reason code; no ID number, image, document URL or extracted ID fields in the attestation. Remove attestation on account deletion unless specifically held.

Job applications

Retention target: Delete 90 days after rejection, withdrawal or vacancy closure; successful applications reduce to necessary booking facts.

Minimum retained and purpose: Aggregate counts only if genuinely non-identifying.

Bookings and jobs, including payment-status records

Retention target: Retain minimal completed arrangement history for 12 months after closure; remove unnecessary addresses, free text and contact details sooner when no longer needed. Open cases use documented holds.

Minimum retained and purpose: Booking reference, dates, parties' restricted references, service category, terminal status and necessary dispute evidence.

Chats, messages and attachments

Retention target: Delete after 90 days of inactivity once associated arrangement/case is closed. For unlinked conversations, 90 days of inactivity. A deletion request can trigger earlier redaction/removal after scoped counterpart/hold review.

Minimum retained and purpose: Only necessary excerpts attached to an active documented case, never the full chat by default.

Reviews, vouches and friendship/trust relationships

Retention target: Remove direct account links and private relationship edges on account deletion. Keep a public review only if all identifying free text/media can be safely removed and its continued purpose is established; otherwise remove it.

Minimum retained and purpose: Truly anonymous rating aggregates may remain; records that can still be linked to your account remain subject to this schedule.

Safety reports, abuse evidence and case-specific audit records

Retention target: Keep during active investigation; default delete/redact 12 months after case closure. Any extension requires a specific recorded necessity, next review date and deletion trigger.

Minimum retained and purpose: Case reference, reason, necessary evidence, resolution and restricted subject references; restrict access to case handlers.

Routine security and application logs

Retention target: Default 30 days for identifiable diagnostic logs; incident-relevant extracts follow the case rule. Avoid message bodies, ID images, passwords and tokens in logs.

Service-provider logs and other provider-held copies may follow separate retention settings. We have not verified all of those settings and do not promise that every provider copy expires within 30 days.

Minimum retained and purpose: Non-identifying service metrics may remain.

Deletion request and completion register

Retention target: Keep minimal evidence 12 months after request closure; pending requests reviewed while open. Then remove contact/UID links unless an active documented case requires them.

Minimum retained and purpose: Request reference, receipt/verification/closure dates, method identifier, categories/action results, exceptions and next review; no passwords/IDs or unnecessary request text.

Backups, exports and QA copies

For backups under our control, our target is a rolling 30-day recovery window. For temporary exports and testing copies under our control, our target is removal when the task is complete and within seven days, unless a documented active incident requires them. We have not verified expiry across all backup systems or service-provider copies, so these targets do not establish when every such copy disappears. We identify copies relevant to a request and record any removal or expiry still awaiting confirmation.

Google Group beta membership

Retention target: Keep while participant chooses beta access and the pilot is active. Review at pilot closure; remove no-longer-needed membership through an approved closure process.

Minimum retained and purpose: Group membership needed for Play eligibility; no marketing reuse.

Retention exceptions and unfinished removal

If information needs to be kept longer for a specific obligation or active case, we record the reason, minimum information needed, access restrictions, review date and disposal condition. We review holds monthly. We explain information retained or still awaiting removal when reporting the outcome of your request. Closing an account does not by itself erase every associated record or backup.

Your privacy choices and rights

You may contact us to request access to or correction of your personal information, object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or request erasure or blocking where applicable. You may also lodge a complaint with the Philippine National Privacy Commission. Contact benlopcon+kasam@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

For account deletion, follow the account-deletion instructions. Account closure and erasure of associated records are separate steps; closing an account does not by itself confirm that every associated record has been erased.
